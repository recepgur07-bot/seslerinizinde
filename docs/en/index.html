Seslerin İzinde

Social awareness media founded by a blind couple. We show what being blind means in everyday life, in our own voice, and our goal is to bring accessibility to as many people as possible.

  • Our YouTube channel
  • Our TikTok account

Our mission

Public understanding of blindness is often built on guesswork and pity. We want to replace that with real experience: we show what a blind person meets on the street, in the kitchen, on public transport, at school, at work and abroad.

Our aim is that viewers act better the next time: they know how to help a blind person, they don't leave a car on the pavement, and they see accessibility as a right, not a favour.

What we make

  • Awareness

    How to help a blind person, obstacles on pavements and public transport, common mistakes.

  • Education

    Using phones and computers with a screen reader; a Mac and VoiceOver course from scratch; exam and university guides.

  • Everyday life

    The real day of a blind couple at home, in the kitchen and in the city.

  • Travel

    Travelling blind in Türkiye and abroad; accessibility compared across countries.
