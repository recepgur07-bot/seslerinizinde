About us

Seslerin İzinde (Turkish for “Following the Sounds”) is a content brand where a blind couple tells their life in their own voice. Our videos show, as it really is, how a blind person's day goes, which tools we use and how, and where things get difficult. Our videos are in Turkish.

What we share

  • Daily life

    How our day goes; small practices that make things easier at home and outside.

  • Accessibility experiences

    Accessible and inaccessible situations we encounter in places, services and the digital world.

  • Technology

    How we use phones, computers and apps with a screen reader.

  • Travel

    Places we visit, how we prepare for trips and what happens on the road.
